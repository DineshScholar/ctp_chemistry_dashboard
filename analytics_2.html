Task Journey Flow (Sankey Diagram)

Visualizes the volume and direction of task routing. Read from left to right: Pod Distribution → Trainer Workload → Final QA State. Thicker bands indicate higher task volumes traversing that specific path.

Cohort Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD)

A stacked area chart showing the current status of tasks based on their original submission date. If the purple (Pending) or orange (Rework) bands expand significantly while green (Approved) shrinks, a bottleneck is occurring in that cohort.
